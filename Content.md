##Topics

* Where to get stock clips?
* How to create image effects?
* Hook: 
  * Direct, to the point. 
  * Options: Example (the context), question, takeaway, setup/context. 
  * Relate it to what the viewer knows. 
  * Specific event/example that illustrates the point. 
* If the story is interesting, don’t need a ton of fanfare/affects. 
* Content should align with the people I want to talk to. 
* Images/clips don’t need to match the audio exactly. 
* On YT, there is a direct correlation between production value and views.  On Tiktok, the correlation is much weaker (or maybe even the opposite). On YT, people want education and entertainment. On tiktok, they want relatability and transparency. 
* Too much data too fast is hard to listen to. 
* Make videos about topics I find interesting. 
* Monologues over cool backgrounds (travel videos).
