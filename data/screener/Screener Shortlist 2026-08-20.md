## AI Infrastructure

Scope note: this pass covers the compute/silicon/networking/data-center-hardware supply chain only. The "Energy" sub-bullet under this theme in `Sectors.md` is deferred to the separate Energy sector pass, to avoid double-counting grid/power-generation names. Memory-primary names (SK hynix, Micron, Sandisk, Silicon Motion, Netlist) are excluded per instruction — too cyclical regardless of AI exposure.

As expected going in: nearly every name that is a *pure, obvious* expression of this theme (NVIDIA, Broadcom, AMD, Arista, Credo, Astera Labs, Lumentum, Coherent, Ciena, TSMC, ASML, Marvell, Arm, Synopsys, Amphenol) is priced in the bottom 1st–15th percentile of the entire universe on EV/Sales — see Tier B. The names that actually clear growth + affordability + risk together are almost all the *picks-and-shovels* layer one step removed from the marquee names: contract manufacturers building the servers/racks, packaging & test equipment for increasingly complex chips, and the cooling/power hardware that gates data center buildout — not the chips or networking gear themselves.

## Energy

Scope note: pure oil & gas E&P and refiners are excluded almost entirely from this pass, per your own thesis — "cheap gas doesn't make the gas producer rich," and refining margins are crack-spread driven, unrelated to AI power demand. Coal is not part of the stated thesis. Solar/renewables are also mostly excluded: your notes frame gas as the near-term answer specifically *because* renewables are intermittent and nuclear is slow — this pass follows that framing rather than second-guessing it. What's left is grouped by which thesis bullet it matches.
