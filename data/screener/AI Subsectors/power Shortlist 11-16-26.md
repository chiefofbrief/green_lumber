Gas producers such as Antero Resources and Range rank high on cash generation and low valuations, and a few tier B names with outstanding figures, Quanta (+37.8%, quarter +60.1%) and EOG (FCF margin 25.0%, P/E 10.0), rank above weaker tier A names. Most regulated utilities sit in the middle on single-digit growth and negative free cash flow from heavy capital spending. The bottom holds companies with shrinking or negligible sales and extreme valuations, such as NuScale (sales -95.7%, EV/sales 167) and Uranium Energy (-44.3%, EV/sales 75).
