Vertiv, Eaton and Quanta lead because they combine strong exposure to the electrical build-out with strong, accelerating sales (Vertiv +35.3% TTM and +61.2% latest quarter; Quanta +37.8% and +60.1%; Eaton +15.5% and +21.4%) and healthy financials. Schneider and Hubbell follow on solid growth at moderate prices (EV/S 1.9x and 4.1x). Several contractors and component makers (nVent, Comfort Systems) grew faster than Eaton or Hubbell but rank below them because electrical is a smaller part of their business. The main trade-offs are price and margin durability. Vicor trades at 14.8x sales, and GE Vernova (FCF 38.9% of sales, 45.6pp above its five-year low), Siemens Energy (gross margin 18.3pp above its low) and Powell (FCF margin 28.2pp above its low) may be earning peak margins. The bottom of the list is diversified groups where electrical equipment is a small share, or companies with slowing, thin or negative growth (Navitas sales -46.4%, Primoris latest quarter -10.7%).
