Antero (+23.9%, +37.9%, P/E 9.4) and the builders of new power capacity, Quanta (+37.8%, +60.1%) and Argan (+29.0%, +61.5%, no debt), follow, with Range, Expand and the other gas producers behind them. Several fast growers rank lower on price or strength: Bloom (+110.6% TTM, but P/E 115.9 and EV/S 15.8), Solaris (FCF margin -95.5%, shares +69.4%) and Venture Global (FCF margin -55.5%). Several companies have margins far above their own 5-year troughs, which may not last: EQT, GE Vernova's FCF, Talen, Argan, Constellation and Vistra. The regulated utilities sit near the bottom because TTM sales growth is mostly under 10% and most have negative free cash flow; the pre-scale nuclear and uranium names (NuScale, UEC) are last on negligible sales.
