The order is led by data holders and data-usability vendors that are growing fast without being priced for perfection. Palantir has the strongest position and growth (+87%, accelerating 40 points) but ranks fourth because EV/sales of 52 leaves no room for a shortfall. Snowflake (+32%) and MongoDB (+25%), both accelerating, are the purest bets on making data usable and on retrieval, and they sit just behind on price. The large data providers hold the most valuable, hardest-to-replicate data but rank in the middle because their sales growth is in single digits: S&P Global's sales fell 5.4% in the latest quarter, and RELX and Verisk grew about 5% last year. Shrinking holders sit at the bottom: Clarivate (-9%), Baidu (-4%) and Pearson (-4%).
