In base, the picks are central generators and gas infrastructure capturing data center demand at reasonable prices: Constellation (sales +37.9%, EV/sales 3.55), Vistra (+19.8%, P/E 16.7), Enterprise Products (quarter +49.3%, EV/sales 1.81), GE Vernova (quarter +26.8%, debt 0.17 years of FCF) and Talen (+70.3%, though debt is 25.1 years of FCF). Bloom Energy has the fastest growth (+110.6%) but misses the top five on an extreme EV/sales of 14.9. Gas producers such as Antero and Range sit just below because gas prices drive their sales as much as volume. In pessimistic, the picks are large companies with backlogs, contracts or low debt at moderate prices: Siemens Energy (debt 0.38 years of FCF, EV/sales 2.32), GE Vernova (debt 0.17 years, though its FCF margin is 45.6 points above its five-year low), Enterprise Products (P/E 12.5), Vistra and Constellation, whose contracted firm power offsets debt of 11.0 and 17.5 years of FCF. Talen and Bloom fall out on heavy debt or extreme valuation. Oil majors such as Chevron and ExxonMobil are strong but owe that strength mostly to oil, and most utilities are held back by negative free cash flow and single-digit growth.
