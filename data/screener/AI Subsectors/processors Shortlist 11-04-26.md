NVIDIA, Broadcom and AMD lead because accelerator volume is the sub-sector's main driver and all three pair it with accelerating sales and healthy margins: TTM sales growth of 83%, 49% and 40%, with latest-quarter growth of 106%, 86% and 50%. NVIDIA's gross and FCF margins sit 17.7 and 27.8 pp above their five-year troughs, so part of its strength may be temporary. Marvell and Cerebras follow on growth (TTM +31% and +91%), but Marvell's growth is slowing and Cerebras burns cash (FCF -133% of sales) at 25.9x sales. The four hyperscalers are cheaper (EV/sales 3.5x to 11.4x against 16.1x to 19.7x for the three leaders), but processors are a small part of each business, so their growth tracks the core business, not chips. Of the four, Microsoft and Meta are financially strong, while Alphabet (FCF 4.2% of sales) and Amazon (FCF -5.9%) are spending heavily. The bottom of the list is the Chinese and holding-company names with weak or shrinking sales and negative free cash flow.
