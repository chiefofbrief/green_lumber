Quanta (PWR), Vertiv (VRT) and Eaton (ETN) lead because they pair a central role in data center electrical demand with fast, accelerating sales: PWR at 37.8% TTM and 60.1% in the latest quarter, VRT at 35.3% and 61.2%, ETN at 20.1% and 28.0%. VRT and ETN pay for that with the highest EV/sales in the top three, 5.9 and 4.92 against PWR's 2.57, and VRT's FCF margin sits 28.4pp above its five-year low. Electrical contractors fill much of the top ten because they combine 22% to 51% TTM growth with low valuations, such as IES (IESC) at 1.64x EV/sales, MYR (MYRG) at 1.04x and EMCOR (EME) at 1.44x. The main trade-offs further down are price and debt: Vicor (VICR) and Analog Devices (ADI) grow fast but trade at 12.19x and 14.38x sales, while cheap names such as WESCO (WCC), MasTec (MTZ) and Primoris (PRIM) carry debt above ten years of free cash flow. Large diversified grid suppliers such as GE Vernova (GEV), Siemens Energy (SMERY) and Hitachi (HTHIY) sit mid-list because their electrical businesses are only part of the company and their margins are well above five-year lows (GEV FCF +45.6pp, SMERY gross +19.8pp). The bottom holds companies with shrinking or flat sales, cash burn, or only an indirect role in the sub-sector.
