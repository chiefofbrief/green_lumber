In base, the picks are the companies that sit where accelerator growth is going: NVIDIA leads on CUDA, 83.4% TTM growth and a P/E of 20.35. Broadcom and Marvell follow as custom ASIC designers, though Marvell's growth is slowing and it trades at a P/E of 52.0. Alphabet comes in on its TPUs, accelerating 22.2% growth and a P/E of 15.26, and AMD on GPUs plus CPUs at 41.9% growth, despite a P/E of 114.96. In pessimistic, NVIDIA and Broadcom still lead on size, FCF margins above 40% and low debt, with NVIDIA's margins far above their five-year lows (gross +17.7pp, FCF +27.8pp) as the main flag. Alphabet holds on its TPU position and scale, and Microsoft on its strength: a 17.3% FCF margin, 2.23x debt and no margin flag. Amazon takes fifth on Trainium and Graviton, a P/E of 21.39 and its $2.88T size, with a -5.9% FCF margin from data center spending that would ease if it cut. AMD drops out, hit on both GPUs and CPUs at a P/E of 114.96. Arm, Lattice and Cerebras fell short on extreme valuations (P/E 216.6 and 357.18, and EV/sales of 24.77 for Cerebras), and Cerebras also on a -210.5% FCF margin. Intel fell short on 13.31x debt to FCF. Meta, Tencent and Qualcomm are strong or cheap but have little processor exposure. The edge AI and Chinese names lacked fit, growth or cash flow.
