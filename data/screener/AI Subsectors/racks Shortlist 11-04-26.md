Server and rack-scale makers lead because demand for the sub-sector follows accelerator volume and they sell the systems. DELL (TTM sales growth 49.2%, latest quarter 57.8%, EV/sales 1.94, FCF margin 5.7%) and SMCI (77.8%, 93.2%, EV/sales 0.53, P/E 9.7) combine the fastest growth in the A tier with low multiples. SMCI's cost is financial: gross margin 10.8%, FCF margin -17.9% and 11.0% share dilution. CLS (61.6%, 82.5%, debt 1.5x FCF) and NVT (48.0%, 43.7%, FCF margin 12.1%) follow with solid finances. SANM, a B-tier contract manufacturer, ranks above HPE (26.6% TTM growth) because its growth is more than double that (58.6% TTM, 69.7% latest quarter) at a lower sales multiple (EV/sales 0.74 versus 2.08). VRT has strong growth but the richest valuation among the established companies (EV/sales 6.79) on margins far above its own history. CBRS grows fastest on paper (90.8%) but trades at EV/sales 25.9 with an FCF margin of -133%. IBM and ENS rank last, with TTM growth of 6.8% and 3.7%.
