### 2026-10-22 | Arista Patches Actively Exploited VeloCloud Zero-Day
*19 articles in cluster*

Arista says CVE-2026-114625, a critical SQL injection vulnerability in VeloCloud Orchestrator, is being actively exploited. Anyone with a valid account, including users with the lowest-privilege Enterprise Read Only role, could escalate to local superuser-operator access, potentially compromising the orchestrator host and managed SD-WAN fabric. On-premises customers should upgrade to version 5.2.3.18, 6.1.3.10 or 6.4.2.12, depending on their release train; hosted and dedicated services have been patched, while unsupported releases remain unassessed. Until patched, administrators should restrict web access to trusted administrative networks, investigate suspicious activity and reported attacker IPs 74.82.197.86 and 38.54.50.151, and preserve logs and system evidence if compromise is suspected. This is the third VeloCloud Orchestrator vulnerability reported exploited in 2026, and fixes for the earlier flaws do not protect against this one.

**Key Points:**
- Arista’s advisory assigns CVE-2026-114625 a CVSS 3.1 score of 9.9 and a CVSS 4.0 score of 9.4, and classifies it as CWE-89. Unlike the two earlier VeloCloud Orchestrator flaws exploited in 2026, this vulnerability requires an authenticated login.
- Arista has not disclosed when exploitation began, which orchestrators were targeted, or what attackers did after gaining access, leaving organizations to assess possible compromise through their own investigations.
- VeloCloud Gateway and VeloCloud Edge are not affected. Release trains outside the advisory’s listed ranges, including version 7.0, are also not listed as vulnerable, but end-of-support releases were not assessed and should be treated as unknown rather than safe.
