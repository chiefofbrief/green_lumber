Staera Semiconductor, formerly New Silicon Corporation, has emerged from stealth with $4.6 million in pre-seed funding led by Openspace Capital to develop optical links built directly into silicon chips. By integrating light sources and optical waveguides into interposer or bridge chips, Staera says its approach could improve data-transfer capacity and energy efficiency while working with existing chip-packaging methods. **Key Points:**
- Staera says Lightwire targets data rates of 4–8 Gbit/s per link, more than 600 links per millimeter of chip edge, and energy use below 0.2 pJ per bit—compared with about 5 pJ per bit for co-packaged optical links.
- The company says it has worked on integrating gallium nitride LEDs into silicon chips through commercial foundries since 2016, and that its manufacturing processes have achieved yields of 98% to 99%.
- Staera originated as a spin-off from the Low Energy Electronic Systems research group at the Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology.
