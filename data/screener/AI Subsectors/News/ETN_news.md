*6 articles in cluster*

BMW and E.ON have expanded their commercial vehicle-to-grid (V2G) program in Germany from the iX3 to the new i3, iX4 and iX5, bringing the eligible lineup to four models equipped with BMW’s sixth-generation eDrive technology. **Key Points:**

- The V2G offer’s tariff includes both a fixed energy price and a standing charge; customers can track their charge level and earned bonus in the My BMW app.
