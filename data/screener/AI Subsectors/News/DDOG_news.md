### 2026-11-12 | MongoDB Patches 26 Flaws, Critical Unauthenticated Crash
*10 articles in cluster*

MongoDB patched 26 vulnerabilities, including critical CVE-2026-118383, a use-after-free flaw rated 9.5 out of 10 that affects replica-set deployments. An unauthenticated attacker can send two specially crafted requests that remain pending until a later failover or catch-up event causes an affected member to crash. Enabling authentication does not block the initial requests, but an attacker cannot directly force the conditions needed to trigger the crash; standalone MongoDB servers are not affected. Datadog Security Labs reproduced the denial-of-service crash but did not demonstrate remote code execution, which MongoDB lists as a possible impact under certain conditions. MongoDB said it is unaware of real-world exploitation, and administrators should install the fixed versions on affected self-managed deployments; Atlas clusters receive fixes through maintenance.

**Key Points:**
- The 26 patched vulnerabilities span several categories, including arbitrary code execution, authentication bypass, privilege escalation, information disclosure and denial of service; the release included two critical and 16 high-severity issues.
- The affected replica-set members include shard and config server members in sharded clusters, not just members of ordinary replica sets.
- The planted notification waiters can persist until a restart, rollback or primary step-down; the attack begins with two ping commands containing readConcern.afterOpTime values.

### 2026-09-24 | Datadog previews AI journey monitoring tools
