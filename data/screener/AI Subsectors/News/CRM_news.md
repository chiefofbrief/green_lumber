### 2026-11-02 | IIT Ropar launches AI wisdom measurement framework
*10 articles in cluster*

A white paper launched at IIT Ropar proposes a framework called HEED for defining and evaluating wisdom in AI, drawing on the authors’ interpretations of the Shiv Sutras and classical Indian texts. Its authors argue that AI systems can perform well on exams yet show sycophancy, often telling users what they want to hear instead of offering sound, beneficial guidance; they cite research finding leading systems sided with users more often than humans in everyday advice. - The white paper was co-authored by Dr Anjali Samani, a former AI and data science executive at Salesforce.

- The framework is initially planned for testing in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
