### 2026-11-09 | AAON Posts Record Sales, Raises 2026 Growth Outlook
*23 articles in cluster*

AAON reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $707.4 million, up 84.1% year over year, and adjusted earnings of $0.83 per share, beating analyst estimates. Strong demand, particularly for BASX products serving data centers, and increased production capacity contributed to the results; the company raised its 2026 sales-growth outlook to 72%–75% and expects gross margins of about 26%. Its backlog increased 40.5% to $1.9 billion. AAON also restated its first- and second-quarter results after finding accounting errors and identified a material weakness in internal financial controls. Shares jumped about 22% in premarket trading after the report, although they had fallen about 25% year to date; analysts’ average 12-month price target was trimmed slightly to $124.60, with the consensus rating remaining Buy.

**Key Points:**
- BASX-branded sales rose 218.3% to $397.2 million, while AAON-branded sales increased 19.6% to $310.2 million.
- Gross profit climbed 76.4% to $188.5 million, and operating income more than doubled, rising 129.3% to $100.0 million. Gross margin reached 26.7%, up 60 basis points sequentially.
- William Blair analysts said BASX orders accelerated and fourth-quarter orders were expected to “meet or exceed” the prior year’s $586 million, citing repeat orders from hyperscaler customers and new customer wins.
