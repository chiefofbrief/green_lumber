Optical and data-center networking names lead because their growth is strong now and sits where the guidance expects the next 1-2 years of demand: optical beyond the rack, then Ethernet switching and short-reach copper. Ciena, Fabrinet and Arista each grew sales 33-36% TTM with the pace speeding up, and Fabrinet is the cheapest of them at 2.3x EV/sales against Arista's 18.9x. Broadcom (+48.7% TTM) and Nvidia (+83.4%) are Tier B because networking is only part of each business, but their scale and financial strength keep them in the top five. Celestica is placed above Lumentum because it is growing nearly as fast (+61.6% vs +83.2% TTM) at 1.7x EV/sales versus 24.9x. The fastest growers with the richest prices (Lumentum, Credo, Astera, AXT) sit in the middle, since their 17-36x EV/sales leaves little room, and AXT and AAOI also burn cash. Slow or shrinking telecom names (Nokia, Lumen, Vistance) are last.
