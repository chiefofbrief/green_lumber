# Tools Shortlist 11-16-26

The order is led by systems where work runs and access points that combine double-digit growth with a reasonable price. ServiceNow (sales +21.8%, FCF margin 30.8%, EV/sales 7.1), Alphabet (+22.2%, accelerating 8.8 pp, P/E 15.3) and Microsoft (+17.9% on $346B of sales) top the list, followed by Atlassian (+26.6%, accelerating 7.1 pp) and Salesforce, which grows only 11.2% but is the named example and trades at 4.8x sales and 18.6x earnings. The main trade-off is price. HubSpot (+20.1% at 2.7x sales) and Figma (+44.4% at 5.6x sales) rank above Visa and Mastercard (about 15% growth at 16-17x sales), and Palantir, the fastest grower at +87.1%, sits 13th because of EV/sales of 51.8. Cloudflare (+35.7%) is held back further by EV/sales of 39.7 and debt above 10x FCF. The bottom of the list is payment processors and data providers with shrinking or low-single-digit sales (Fiserv -1.7%, Coinbase -26.6%, StoneCo -17.8%) or weak balance sheets (Euronet with debt above 10x FCF, PagSeguro and Lightspeed with negative FCF). Large gaps above five-year margin troughs (Adyen, SailPoint, Coinbase, StoneCo, Palantir, GitLab) are treated as one concern each.
