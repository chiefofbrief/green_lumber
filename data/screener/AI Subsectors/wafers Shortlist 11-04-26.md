The order is led by companies that are central to wafer or advanced packaging capacity and whose sales are growing fast and speeding up: TSMC (sales +36.1% TTM, +50.9% latest quarter), Lam (+33.4%, +54.4%), ASE (+26.4%, +44.8%) and Kulicke & Soffa (+44.4%, +122.6%). Teradyne (+74.9% TTM) grows faster than any tier A name, but test sits downstream of wafers and packaging and it is a B, so it ranks seventh. Price and cash are the main trade-offs: KLA, ASML and Advantest trade at 14.3x, 13.4x and 16.6x sales; ASML's 53.6% FCF margin is 41.8 pp above its five-year low and Advantest's 32.2% is 29.7 pp above; ASE and ACM Research are burning cash (FCF margins -14.1% and -11.6%) to fund growth. The mature-node foundries (UMC, GlobalFoundries, Tower) have limited exposure to AI wafers, and only UMC ranks high, on +25.5% quarterly growth and a P/E of 16.0. Lasertec (sales -18.6% TTM) and DuPont (-32.7%) are last on shrinking sales.
