# Strength

Inputs and output for the Strength pass. Figures are computed by `run_financials.py` from FMP statements fetched November 24, 2026 (raw files in `raw/`). TTM is the last four reported quarters, or the last two half-years for half-yearly reporters (LSEGY, RELX, SBGSY). SBC (% of sales, TTM) is stock-based compensation over sales; a dash means FMP reports none, usually because the company does not break it out in its quarterly cash flow statement. FCF margin (last 5 FY) is free cash flow over sales for each of the last five fiscal years, oldest first. Share count change compares the basic weighted average share count in the latest period with the same period a year earlier. Debt / OCF is total debt (FMP's figure, which includes lease liabilities) divided by TTM operating cash flow, in years. Net debt or net cash is total debt minus cash and short-term investments, from the latest balance sheet, in the company's reporting currency. Gross margin vs 5-yr low is from the screener (mid-November 2026), as used in the Valuation pass. A dash means the figure is not available. Rows 131 onward were fetched November 27, 2026. Kinetik (KNTK) and Solaris (SEI) report only one of their two share classes, so their share count rises when holders of the other class convert, with no new shares sold (their cash flow statements show no stock issuance); their share count change likely overstates dilution.
