The order is driven mainly by growth, and in networking the best positioned names are the optical ones beyond the rack. Lumentum leads on laser exposure and the fastest, still accelerating sales growth among the optical names. Ciena and Fabrinet follow on strong, accelerating growth and valuations that are lower than most of the group. The biggest trade-offs are price and margin quality. Credo, Astera, Marvell and Broadcom grow fast but trade at 20x to 40x sales with margins well above their own history. AAOI and AXT grow quickly but burn cash or are small and speculative. Copper and DSP names rank below the optical leaders because the guidance treats those as more exposed to co-packaged optics. Contract manufacturers (Celestica, Sanmina) and the connector and enterprise networking names sit lower because networking is only part of their business or their growth is slower. The bottom of the list is shrinking or slow growing businesses (Vistance, Lumen), or enterprise equipment with little AI exposure.
