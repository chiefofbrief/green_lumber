### 2026-08-25 | Study shows CAPEX edge for T-BOSS shaftline Suezmax
*6 articles in cluster*

A series of studies on a 274-meter Suezmax tanker with a 670-mm shaft diameter consistently find that Thordon Bearings’ T-BOSS sterntubeless shaftline delivers design, operational, and economic advantages over traditional oil-lubricated sterntubes. Collectively, the findings suggest that the sterntubeless system can offer lifetime economic and sustainability benefits, with broader implications for shipowners seeking more versatile and compliant vessels.

**Key Points:**
- Sterntubeless T-BOSS design includes a dry inspection chamber that lets engineers inspect and replace bearings without withdrawing the propeller shaft or removing the propeller.
- The T-BOSS seawater-lubricated solution eliminates sterntube lubricating oil purchases and reduces regulatory costs by avoiding the need for Environmentally Acceptable Lubricants (EALs).
- The CAPEX comparison evaluates all shipbuilding disciplines—sealing systems, piping, structural works, shafting, outfitting, painting, electrical systems, testing, and regulatory aspects—and still shows lower labour and installation costs with the T-BOSS system.
